    Personal Projects

    BELT NINE

    A first-person game about mining asteroids, written in vanilla JavaScript.
    No engine, no libraries, no build step while developing — and no assets at all: the belt is generated from a seed, the soundtrack is played by a sequencer rather than off a file, and nothing is ever loaded.
    Shoot a rock until it comes apart, fill your hold, fly it back to the mothership. Up to four pilots on one screen, and no network anywhere.
    The same scene can be drawn two ways — glowing wireframe or dithered pixels — and you can switch mid-flight. I wrote about how that works here.

    SneakBit

    Started as a side project to learn Rust, it's now a fully-fledged game.
    I recently rewritten it completely in vanilla Js and have been adding a ton of features, including online multiplayer!
    You can play co-op on a split screen, or online over WebSocket and WebRTC, with the host owning the game state.
    The game is a story-driven RPG, with aesthetic and mechanics heavily influenced by OG GameBoy games.
    I personally made everything from scratch, including the engine and all the sprite (except for fonts, music and sound effects).
    The project is open source, and you can play it now in your browser — or buy it on Steam if you'd like to support it! :)

    Pipper

    Pipper is a web browser in its simplest possible form: a webview and exactly 6 buttons.
    Its only distinctive feature is the ability to float over other windows, allowing content to be always visible.
    Some early users have started to use it as a control panel for audio mixers, among other things.
    The project is open source and available on the App Store.
