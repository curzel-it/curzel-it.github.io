Services and Definitions

This Privacy Policy applies to some apps and services developed by Federico Curzel, including:

  • Desktop Pets — app for iOS and macOS.
  • MyRentalShop — app for iPad.
  • Pinball — game for Android and iOS.
  • Unlimited Radio — app for Android and iOS. A procedural music generator that runs entirely offline and on-device. Uses a VAE model trained on publicly available datasets (including Sage and based on jacbz/lofi) to generate music in real time.
  • Retro Deck — app for Android and iOS. A card game that uses a local LLM (currently Qwen 0.5B with publicly available weights from Hugging Face; model may change in future updates) running entirely offline and on-device. Card artwork is original and designed by Federico Curzel. Physical decks are available at curzel.it/retro_deck.

It also applies to apps and services developed by Hidden Mugs (alias for Federico Curzel), including:

  • SneakBit — game for Android, iOS, Windows, Linux, and macOS.

These software and services are provided as either free or paid, as specified in individual products, and are offered as is without warranty of any kind. Additional services might be provided at a cost; please refer to indications within individual products.

SneakBit is a paid game.
Pinball, Unlimited Radio, and Retro Deck are free apps supported by ads and in-app purchases.
Unlimited Radio and Retro Deck work entirely offline; no user data is transmitted for music generation or card gameplay.

Information Collection and Use

To enhance user experience, I may collect specific types of data, including:

  • Device-related information, such as IP address, operating system, and app version.
  • Usage data, such as app activity logs, crash reports, and performance metrics.

Log Data

In case of errors, I may collect Log Data via third-party services. This data may include device-related information such as:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address.
  • Device name and operating system version.
  • App configuration details at the time of the error.
  • Time and date of use, along with usage statistics.

My apps do not explicitly use cookies. However, third-party libraries integrated into the apps may use cookies to improve their functionality. You can choose to accept or refuse these cookies via your device settings. Note that refusing cookies may limit app functionality.
