HOW IT WORKS

A neural network (VAE) running entirely on your device generates chord progressions and melodies from a random seed. A composition engine arranges them into full tracks with piano, guitar, bass, drums, and effects. Everything is created in real time — nothing is pre-recorded.

The model is trained on publicly available datasets including Sage and based on jacbz/lofi.
