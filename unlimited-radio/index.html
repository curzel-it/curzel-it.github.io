Unlimited Radio

Unlimited Radio generates music, on the fly, comepltely offline, on your iPhone or iPad. No accounts, no ads, no subs. Save tracks you like as audio files.

How it works

It's a procedural synthesizer!

What it plays

Synthwave, psychedelic rock, Berlin school and lofi hip-hop. Also some vocals!
