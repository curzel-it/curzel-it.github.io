Flying dragon racing

Raise dragons from the egg, race them through the skies and watch every race broadcast live.

Early previewPlay in your browser Coming soonSteam · App Store · Google Play

Broadcast live

A drone camera follows the field and a director picks the cuts: the lead change, the comeback, the photo finish, with race control calling it all.

Take the reins

Watch from the stands or ride your own dragon: steer it through the course and fight for the line.

Raise them

Every dragon starts as an egg. Warm it, hatch it, care for it a few times a day and watch it grow from kid to teen to adult, up the age leagues to the Main Event.

Make new ones

Bring your adult dragons to the Soul Altar for a chance at a new egg. Heads, horns, wings, tails and colours pass on to the young, so no two dragons look alike.

Early preview

Free The early preview plays in your browser, on phone or desktop. The game is still in development, so expect changes, and your stable may be reset along the way.

Your save stays on your device; an optional account keeps it in step across devices and puts it on the leaderboards.

Dragons! is coming to Steam, the App Store and Google Play.
