What you do out there

Asteroids are terrain rather than targets: shoot one long enough and it comes apart into ore and upgrades, while the gun overheats and the hull doesn't grow back. A full hold is worth nothing until it's docked at the mothership, and the flight home is the part of the run you can lose.

Made of nothing

The belt is grown from a seed and every note of the soundtrack is played by a sequencer, so there isn't one asset in the build — no sprites, no models, no audio files. It's vanilla JavaScript and a canvas, with no runtime libraries and three renderers behind one interface: pixels, wireframe or characters, swappable mid-flight.
